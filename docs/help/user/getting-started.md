# Getting started

Welcome to the Test & Measurement station. This guide gets you from login to your first test.

## Sign in
1. Open the station in your browser.
2. Enter your **username** and **password**.
3. First login with a temporary password forces a **password change** before you can continue.

If you are locked out, ask a super_admin to reset your password.

## The layout
- **Top bar** — logo (click to return to the Dashboard), station-readiness lamp, light/dark toggle, your session menu, and the **?** help button (this help).
- **Side menu** — the screens you have permission to use. What you see depends on your role.

## Your role
Each user has one **role** (operator, engineer, maintenance, admin, super_admin). The role decides which screens and actions are available. You only see what you can use.

## First test
1. Go to **Runs** (Test Bench).
2. Scan or type a **barcode**, or pick a **recipe**.
3. Press **Start**. Watch the live results and the big **PASS / FAIL** verdict.

See [Test Bench](./test-bench.md) for details.
